About music

Loyal9 LLC · Last updated: 2026

music is a free, open-licensed music platform. No account. No ads. No subscriptions. Just music — … tracks you can stream, explore, and save, free forever.

The Music

Every track on this platform is sourced from one of five open-licensed archives. All content is either in the public domain or released under a Creative Commons license. Nothing here is pirated. Nothing here costs money to listen to.

Internet Archive

archive.org

One of the world's largest digital libraries of freely available media. We index music, podcasts, lectures, audiobooks, and historical recordings across seven collections — netlabels, 78rpm, opensource_audio, audio_music, georgeblood, audio_bookspoetry, and librivoxaudio.

Jamendo

jamendo.com

A platform for independent artists releasing music under Creative Commons licenses. We index tracks across rock, electronic, ambient, jazz, hip-hop, folk, classical, and more.

Freesound

freesound.org

A collaborative database of CC-licensed audio. We index music tracks (30s+) across genre tags including jazz, classical, electronic, ambient, folk, and more.

Wikimedia Commons

commons.wikimedia.org

The free media repository behind Wikipedia. We index audio files from genre categories including classical, jazz, blues, folk, electronic, and more — all public domain or CC-licensed.

ccMixter

ccmixter.org

A community music remixing site built entirely on Creative Commons licensing. Originals, remixes, samples, and a cappellas — all free to stream.

How It Works

Search by artist, album, or song title. Browse by genre, mood, or content type. Filter results by artist and album. Stream directly in your browser. Save tracks to your library. It works offline as a PWA — install it to your home screen and it behaves like a native app.

No login required. Your library and listening history are stored locally on your device and never sent anywhere.

Analytics

This platform uses Google Analytics and Google Search Console. These tools collect anonymous, aggregate usage data — pages visited, search queries used, general location (country/region). This data is used solely to understand how the platform is being used so it can be improved. No personal identity data is collected. No data is sold or shared with third parties. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
