Privacy Policy

Loyal9 LLC · Last updated: 2026

music is operated by Loyal9 LLC, Riverside, CA. This policy explains what data is collected, why, and how it's handled. We keep it short because we mean it.

What We Collect

Analytics data — this platform uses Google Analytics and Google Search Console. These services collect anonymous, aggregate data including pages visited, search queries entered, session duration, and general geographic region (country/city level). This data is used solely to understand how the platform is used and to improve it.

Local storage — your library (saved tracks), recently played tracks, and playback queue are stored in your browser's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted to any server.

What We Do Not Collect

  • No account registration — no name, email, or password is ever collected
  • No identity data of any kind
  • No payment information
  • No tracking pixels or third-party ad networks
  • No cookies beyond what Google Analytics sets for session tracking
  • No audio listening history transmitted off your device

Google Analytics

Google Analytics is used to collect anonymous usage statistics. Google may use this data in accordance with their own privacy policy. You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

The Music

All music streamed through this platform is sourced from the Internet Archive. Audio streams are served directly from Internet Archive servers. We do not proxy, store, or re-host any audio content.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties for commercial purposes. The only third-party data processor used is Google Analytics, described above.

Children's Privacy

This service is a general-audience music platform and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect personal data, there is nothing to request deletion of. Your locally stored library and history can be cleared at any time through your browser's settings (clear site data / localStorage).

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the platform after changes constitutes acceptance.
